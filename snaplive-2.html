SnapLive 2.0

A live-simulcasting platform that lets sellers broadcast once and go live across China's biggest marketplaces — Douyin, Xiaohongshu/RED, and Taobao Live. I designed the end-to-end experience across web, tablet, and mobile, mobile-first for buyers and sellers.

Overview

SnapLive 2.0 is a live-simulcasting platform. Where most live-selling tools stream to a single channel, SnapLive lets a seller go live once from LiveCam and broadcast to several live-selling platforms at the same time — Douyin, Xiaohongshu/RED, and Taobao Live — while LiveHub handles orders, inventory, and fulfilment behind the scenes. That simulcast-to-many capability is its core differentiator.

I joined as the product designer and owned the end-to-end UI/UX — from marketplace research and user flows to a full high-fidelity system. The work was mobile-first for buyers and sellers, built on shadcn/ui, and shaped by Ant Design conventions and Chinese live-selling UI patterns so it would feel native to its market.

Brand & interface foundations

The SnapLive 2.0 wordmark anchors a compact, high-contrast identity that reads at broadcast scale — shipped in landscape and boxed lockups with green-blue, yellow, and red variants. On top of it sits a shadcn/ui component library tuned with Ant Design density and Chinese-market patterns: a persistent left rail, marketplace toggles, and an in-stream AI "Shopping Buddy."

Outcome

In roughly four months, SnapLive 2.0 went from a prototype ticket to a full high-fidelity, bilingual (EN/KO) design system spanning ~40 screens across desktop, tablet, and mobile — buyer and seller journeys, LiveCam broadcasting with multi-camera and simulcast presets, LiveConnect to three Chinese marketplaces, and LiveHub order and inventory management. The design translated Ant Design conventions and Chinese live-selling patterns into a shadcn/ui system the team is building in Next.js.
