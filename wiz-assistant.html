WIZ Assistant

W Labs' work-support AI assistant — a "smart information desk" you embed in any website to answer questions, guide users step-by-step, and handle document requests. I designed its promotion page and produced the promo video.

Overview

WIZ Assistant is one of W Labs' digital products — a work-support AI assistant that embeds directly into a website (or runs standalone) as a friendly "smart information desk." It answers user questions immediately, walks people through step-by-step procedures, and even handles document requests like meeting minutes and company profiles — positioned as practical AI that takes work off your plate, not a flashy chatbot.

My role was the product's outward face: I designed the promotion/landing page — positioning, use-case demos, pricing tiers, and the WIZ mascot — and produced the promo video that tells its story. It shipped as an embeddable chatbot (hosted at wiz.wsoft.space) and as the "Personal Service Assistant" living on the W Labs site.
