TMC — Print & Publishing E-commerce

A Korean print-and-publishing e-commerce platform with quote-to-order flows for perfect-bound books, catalogs, flyers, and NCS learning modules. Taken from research and personas to a full bilingual high-fidelity system — validated with usability testing and client feedback, then rebuilt in a top-to-bottom redesign.

Overview

TMC is a print-and-publishing e-commerce platform for the Korean market. Buyers configure and order printed products — perfect-bound books, catalogs and brochures, leaflets and flyers, and NCS learning modules — through guided quote-to-order flows, while an admin side manages orders, production, and fulfilment.

I owned the UI/UX end to end: competitive analysis, three user personas, and information architecture up front; a design system and high-fidelity screens; AI-assisted usability testing and multiple client-feedback rounds; and a full English/Korean rollout — followed by a complete UI/UX renewal that rebuilt the product from the ground up.

Research & foundations

The work started from evidence, not aesthetics: a review of the client's sitemap, a competitive analysis of Korean print-commerce sites, and three personas built from the target audience. Those fed the information architecture and a design system that kept the multi-product, bilingual experience consistent.

Outcome

TMC grew from a research-led design into a comprehensive, bilingual (EN/KO) e-commerce system spanning the full catalog of print products across web, tablet, and mobile — guided quote-to-order flows, live order tracking, an admin panel, and email templates. Validated through AI usability testing and multiple client-feedback rounds, it was then rebuilt in a full UI/UX renewal that the team demoed and shipped into implementation.
