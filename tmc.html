TMC — Print & Publishing E-commerce

A Korean print-and-publishing e-commerce platform with quote-to-order flows for perfect-bound books, catalogs, flyers, and NCS learning modules. Taken from research and personas to a full bilingual high-fidelity system — validated with usability testing and client feedback, then rebuilt in a top-to-bottom redesign.

Overview

TMC is a print-and-publishing e-commerce platform for the Korean market. Buyers configure and order printed products — perfect-bound books, catalogs and brochures, leaflets and flyers, and NCS learning modules — through guided quote-to-order flows, while an admin side manages orders, production, and fulfilment.

I owned the UI/UX end to end: competitive analysis, three user personas, and information architecture up front; a design system and high-fidelity screens; AI-assisted usability testing and multiple client-feedback rounds; and a full English/Korean rollout — followed by a complete UI/UX renewal that rebuilt the product from the ground up. I also prototyped the customer and admin front-ends with Claude Code (AI-assisted “vibe-coding”), taking the designs into working interfaces.

Those fed the information architecture and a design system that kept the multi-product, bilingual experience consistent.

From design to working prototype

To pressure-test the flows, I took the designs into a working front-end — vibe-coded with Claude Code in React 19 + Vite + Tailwind over a lightweight Express API. It's dual-sided: a customer portal (Request Quote → Cart → Orders → History, plus Account and NicePay checkout) and an admin portal for providing quotations, reviewing uploaded files, advancing order status, and managing users — each of the five product types with its own specifications modal.

Outcome

TMC grew from a research-led design into a comprehensive, bilingual (EN/KO) e-commerce system spanning the full catalog of print products across web, tablet, and mobile — guided quote-to-order flows, live order tracking, an admin panel, and email templates. Validated through AI usability testing and multiple client-feedback rounds, it was then rebuilt in a full UI/UX renewal that the team demoed and shipped into implementation.
